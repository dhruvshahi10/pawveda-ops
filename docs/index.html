How it works, in the middle of the night.

1

Describe what you're seeing.

Off his food, limping, ate something off the street.

2

It reads your pet's record.

Age, weight, meds, allergies, vaccines. Every answer is for your pet, not a generic pet.

3

You get one of three answers.

Emergency

Go to a vet now. You get the immediate steps.

Visit soon

Book the vet. You get what to watch until then.

Monitor

Keep an eye on it. You get what to watch, and the reasoning.

A rules engine on your phone runs alongside the AI. If they disagree, the more cautious answer wins. No signal? Triage still works offline, and says so.
